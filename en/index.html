Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Package: com.wejigsaw · Last updated: 2026-07-21

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what data the puzzle game We Jigsaw ("the app") collects and how it is handled. The app works without any sign-up or login and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.

2. Data stored only on your device (never sent to servers)

  • Game progress, completion state, best times: stored locally on your device via Android DataStore.
  • Settings: your selected language and haptics (vibration) preference are stored on-device.
  • "My Photos" puzzles: photos chosen through the system photo picker are stored only on your device and are never uploaded.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

3. Data handled by third parties (ads)

The app uses Google AdMob to show an app-open ad and a bottom banner ad. To serve and measure ads, the AdMob SDK may collect/process:

  • Advertising ID (or device identifiers), coarse device/location info, usage statistics
  • App interactions, crash/diagnostic data

This data is sent to and processed by Google. See the Google Privacy Policy and AdMob policies. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's "Opt out of Ads Personalization" setting.

4. Permissions used

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to load ads.
  • VIBRATE: haptic feedback when pieces snap.

Photos are accessed only via the system photo picker, so no storage/camera permissions are requested.

5. Children & Families

The app is not directed to children under 13. Any Families-policy advertising rules follow the target-audience setting in the Google Play Console. If we learn a child has provided unintended information, we will delete it.

6. Data retention & deletion

All on-device data is removed when you uninstall the app (or clear app data). Data already sent to the ad service (Google) is retained/deleted according to Google's policies.

7. Your choices

  • Ad personalization: opt out in device settings.
  • Haptics: can be turned off in the app settings.
  • Local data: delete instantly by uninstalling or clearing app data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; changes will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date.
